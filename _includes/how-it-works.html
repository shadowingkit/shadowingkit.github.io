How ShadowingKit Works

1

Choose Your Content

Select a curated episode matched to your level, or import your own Spanish content from TikTok, Instagram, or any video file.

2

Listen and Analyze

Play through the audio with the full transcript visible. Get familiar with the speaker's pace, rhythm, and pronunciation before you shadow.

3

Shadow Out Loud

Speak simultaneously with the native speaker. Follow the highlighted text to stay in sync. Sentence-by-sentence navigation lets you repeat any phrase until it feels natural.

4

Progress to Blind Shadowing

As your confidence builds, practice without the text. Your brain has to process and produce Spanish purely by ear. This is the stage that builds true automaticity.
