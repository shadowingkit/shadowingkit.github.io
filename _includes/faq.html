Frequently Asked Questions

About the Shadowing Technique

What is the shadowing technique?

Shadowing is a technique where you speak simultaneously with a native speaker, mimicking their rhythm, intonation, and pronunciation in real time. Originally developed for conference interpreter training, it builds speech automaticity, the ability to produce language without conscious translation.

How is it different from just listening and repeating?

In standard listen-and-repeat, you stop the audio, think about what you heard, then say it, which keeps the analytical brain in control. In shadowing, you speak simultaneously with the audio, forcing your brain to bypass conscious translation and produce speech as a reflex. This is what builds real fluency.

How long until I see results?

Research shows noticeable pronunciation gains within 4–6 weeks of consistent 10–15 minute daily sessions. Comprehension improvements appear even earlier, typically within 2–3 weeks.

How much time per day do I need?

10–15 minutes of focused shadowing per day is the research-backed sweet spot. Consistency matters far more than session length, daily short sessions outperform occasional long ones.

Do I need to understand the content to shadow it?

No, and that's part of what makes shadowing powerful. You train your phonological system even while processing meaning. That said, ShadowingKit's curated library is structured by proficiency level so you can start with content appropriate to your comprehension.

About ShadowingKit

Is the app good for complete beginners?

Yes. The app includes a dedicated A2 entry path called "Spanish in Two Minutes", 50+ short, slow-paced episodes designed to prevent cognitive overload for upper beginners. You need basic vocabulary, but you don't need to be intermediate to start.

What's the difference between the curated library and my own content?

The curated library is recorded by a native speaker at controlled pacing, structured by proficiency level, and covers topics designed to be engaging and culturally relevant. Your own content lets you shadow anything that interests you, a TikTok creator you follow, a podcast, a TV show, auto-transcribed and formatted the same way.

How does the auto-transcription work?

You import an audio or video file (or record audio directly in the app), and the app automatically generates a synchronized transcript using speech recognition. The result is the same highlighted-text shadowing experience as the curated content.
