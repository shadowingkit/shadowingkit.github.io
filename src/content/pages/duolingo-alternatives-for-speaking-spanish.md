# Duolingo Alternatives for Speaking Spanish: An Honest Guide

> **In short:** People leave Duolingo because the streak keeps growing and the speaking never starts. The right alternative depends on which skill you're missing. italki gives you live conversation with a tutor. Pimsleur gives you a structured audio course. Dreaming Spanish builds listening comprehension. ShadowingKit trains pronunciation and speaking reflexes through shadowing. Whatever you pick, make sure it forces you to produce Spanish out loud, every session.

You have a 300-day streak and you still freeze when a waiter asks you a question. That gap is the single most common reason learners search for a Duolingo alternative.

The gap has a simple cause. Duolingo trains recognition: tap the right word, match the pair, fill the blank. Speaking is production: retrieve words, build grammar, and move your mouth in real time. Recognition practice does little for production. A Michigan State University study of Duolingo learners (Loewen et al., 2019) found the app's gains concentrated in vocabulary and comprehension, with speaking the weakest area.

So the question is never "which app is best". It's "which tool trains the skill I'm missing".

---

## What a Real Speaking Alternative Must Have

Filter any app or method through four requirements:

- **You speak out loud in every session.** Silent tapping trains tapping.
- **Native-speed audio.** Slowed, robotic audio teaches a Spanish nobody speaks.
- **Feedback or a model to match.** You need a reference for rhythm and pronunciation.
- **Fits your level.** A2 learners need short and structured. B1-B2 learners need authentic content.

Most popular apps fail the first requirement. That includes Duolingo, Anki, and most grammar apps.

| **italki / Preply** | Live conversation with a tutor | Learners ready to converse | Cost per hour, scheduling, pressure |

Each of these is stronger than Duolingo for its specific skill. None of them covers everything, and that's fine. You're fixing a specific gap.

---

## Match the Tool to Your Gap

- **"I know words but conversations terrify me."** Book italki sessions. Nothing replaces live conversation once you can survive it.
- **"I'm not ready to talk to a person yet."** This is where shadowing fits. You speak along with native audio, out loud, with zero judgment and no partner. It builds the reflexes that make later conversation survivable.
- **"I understand almost nothing by ear."** Build input first with Dreaming Spanish or podcasts, then add production.
- **"I want one structured course."** Pimsleur, then add authentic content when it starts feeling scripted.

Many learners combine two: comprehension input plus daily shadowing covers both directions of fluency for less than the cost of one tutor hour per month.
